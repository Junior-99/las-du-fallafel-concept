# Le Marais poster energy

Forest green, acid yellow and pink. Huge outlined Archivo Black display text, food photography in an arched frame, sticker motifs, bold rules and a moving street-poster ticker. Dedicated French/English food guide, photo wall, story, visit planner and workspace. Real food imagery carries the design; a playful 3D pita illustrates scroll motion without claiming actual food geometry. Original layout and code; no paid template copied.
